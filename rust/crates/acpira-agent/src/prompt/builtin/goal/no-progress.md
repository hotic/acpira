about: Between a resumed or continued goal turn's objective and the rules
---
No-progress check: classify the previous goal turn as progress, a verified wait, or no progress. Progress changes authoritative state, completes work, or yields evidence that changes the next action; restating status or planning without acting is no progress. A verified wait polls a specific job or process confirmed live now (a background command through the job tool), not a memory of one; a timeout while watching it is not its end. After a turn without progress, revalidate and take the next safe action; if none exists because the same genuine blocker remains, report it and leave the goal active until the blocked audit below is met.
